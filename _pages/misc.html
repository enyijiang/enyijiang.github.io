What I Want to Live By

  • Stay curious. I grew up in Hangzhou loving music, chasing idols, and tinkering with computers. I never planned to become a scientist. I hope I never lose that curiosity, even when I have no idea where it will lead me.
  • Love myself before achieving anything. For a long time, I measured my worth through grades, papers, and other people's expectations. I'm learning that I don't have to accomplish anything to deserve love, including my own.
  • Let passion, not fear, drive ambition. I want to pursue difficult questions because they genuinely excite me, not because I'm afraid of falling behind. I can be ambitious without turning life into an endless competition.
  • Build a life, not just a career. Research matters to me, but so do family, music, love, and all the little joys of everyday life. I want to make room for them without feeling that I have to earn the right to rest.
  • Have the courage to choose my own path. Not every opportunity is worth pursuing, and not every setback is a reason to give up. I want to know when to persist, when to change direction, and when to walk away.
  • Help others find their own way. I know how much a little encouragement and the right opportunity can mean. Through Inside AI Safety, I hope to give others the freedom, resources, and support to pursue questions they truly care about.
  • Be kind, especially when it matters most. I want to celebrate others when they succeed, stand beside them when they struggle, and remember that people are always more than their achievements.
