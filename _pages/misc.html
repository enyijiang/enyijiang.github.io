What I Want to Live By

  • Growing up in Hangzhou, I loved music and tinkering with computers. I want to keep following that kind of curiosity, even when I have no idea where it will take me.
  • I used to look to my grades, and later to my papers, to tell me how I was doing as a person. I’m learning that I’m just as worthy of love on the days I have nothing to show.
  • I want a life with room for research, family, and music. I get to decide how much room each takes, and I can change my mind.
  • When research gets hard, I give myself time to ask what went wrong, what support I need, and whether the question still matters to me.
  • When I mentor someone, I want to ask, “What would you try if you had the chance?” Through Inside AI Safety, I want to help them find the compute, collaborators, or encouragement to try it.
