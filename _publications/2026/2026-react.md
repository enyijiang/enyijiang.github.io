abstract: >-
  REACT separates risk readability from effective control: across three distilled reasoning models, a single hidden-state intervention is most effective within an early two- to three-token window. On DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B, it raises fully safe chain-of-thought and answer outputs on StrongREJECT-273 from 29.06% to 82.05% while keeping benign refusal and usefulness near baseline.
